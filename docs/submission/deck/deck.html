The insight

Videos make you do the hardest part in your head.

1. You have to mirror everything

The signer faces you, so their right hand is on your left. Hearing non-signers copying a face-to-face model made errors on 24.3% of sideways movements. In a second study, a model that matched their mirroring cut those errors from 20.6% to 0.9%.

2. Nobody checks your hands

A video can't see that your ring finger is up or your hand is at your cheek instead of your chin. Parents practise alone, late at night, and never know if they got it right.

A headset can help with both: put guide hands exactly where yours are, and read 25 joints per hand.

Shield & Meier 2018, Frontiers in Psychology. Parents' barriers: time, cost, access (Lieberman et al. 2024).

A reason to come back

Your garden is your progress.

  • Plants grow as signs move into long-term memory (spaced repetition graded by how the attempt went, not by self-rating).
  • They droop when a review is due, and a poke replays the sign.
  • Five-minute sessions, streaks, a daily goal.
  • Guide hands fade after two unaided successes, so skill transfers.

Technical core

Signs are data, not video.

// THANK-YOU, written the way linguists describe signs
oneHanded(
  { id: 'thank-you', gloss: 'THANK-YOU' },
  { shape: 'open-B', at: 'chin',
    contact: 'fingertips', palm: 'in' },
  { to: { at: 'chin', offset: [0.03, -0.12, -0.22],
          palm: 'up-in' }, path: 'arc' },
);

One definition drives:

  • Sprout's animation and the first-person guide hands
  • The dotted guide path and the written instructions
  • The step-by-step checker
  • Left-handed mirroring, automatically

50 handshapes solved with forward and inverse kinematics from a real Quest hand capture. No training data needed per sign.

Built for the headset

Hands only. Seated. A link, not an install.

Hands-first

Every button (about 2.8–4.5 cm tall) works with a fingertip poke, a pinch ray or look-to-select. Far rays switch off while you sign, so a pinch-like handshape doesn't click by accident.

Airplane-seat test

The stage recenters on your head, the table follows your eye height, everything sits within two feet. Pause and resume follow the headset.

Fast and light

Meta Immersive Web SDK 1.0 and WebXR Hand Input. Procedural, instanced scene; the garden draws ~90 plants in a handful of draw calls.

Accessible by default

Designed for every body that wants to learn.

  • Captions for every prompt, optional voice
  • Left-handed mode: every sign mirrors, the panel moves left
  • "Limited finger range" and "Continue with one hand"
  • Adjustable strictness and guide speed
  • High contrast, calm motion, colour plus shape

Respect for the Deaf community

  • A practice partner, never a translator
  • The site links to Deaf-led resources and Deaf Mentor programs
  • We compared each sign with Handspeak and Lifeprint. No Deaf signer has reviewed Signsprout yet; paid review by Deaf signers comes before launch
  • Hand data never leaves the headset; facial grammar is mentioned as a tip, not scored
