Signsprout

Learn your first ASL signs with your own two hands.

A seated, hands-only Meta Quest app that teaches first ASL vocabulary to families in five-minute sessions. Sprout shows each sign face to face, glowing guide hands sit on your hands, the app checks each finger, and every sign grows your garden.

ProductivityNew ExperienceWebXR · IWSDK 1.0
The problem

Most deaf babies are born to parents who don't sign yet.

Video lessons make parents flip every movement in their heads, and nothing checks their hands. Time and cost are the top barriers.

90%+of deaf children have hearing parents
1 in 4families regularly sign at home
24.3%sideways-move errors by non-signers copying face to face
What makes it different

Guide hands, exactly where yours are.

  • First-person guide hands remove the mental left-right flip; they fade as you improve.
  • Checked in order: handshape, then place, then movement, then the final handshape, with plain-word tips per finger.
  • A garden that is your progress: spaced repetition, plants droop when a review is due.
  • Sprout, the AI coach, plans sessions around real moments using only signs Signsprout can check.
Four steps, about a minute a sign
1 · WatchSprout shows the sign, mirrored for easy copying
2 · TogetherPut your hands inside the glowing hands
3 · Your turnFrom memory, with specific feedback
4 · CelebrateA new plant sprouts in your garden
Built for the headset
  • Hands only end to end: poke, pinch or look at every button (about 2.8–4.5 cm tall), no controller.
  • Seated within two feet; recenters on your head; clean pause and resume.
  • A link, not an install: Meta Immersive Web SDK 1.0 + WebXR Hand Input.
  • 87 signs as linguistic data (handshape, location, palm, movement); one definition drives Sprout, guide, instructions and checker.
  • Accessible: captions, optional voice, left-handed mode, limited finger range, one-hand option, high contrast, calm motion.

Business

Planned: Free core units · Family $7.99/mo or $59/yr (up to 4 members) · Family Pass free for families of deaf children under 3, funded through partner programs · Programs from $39 per family per year.

Beachhead: ~5,600 new US hearing families of deaf babies each year. Expansion: ASL is the #3 language in US colleges; Lingvano reached 2.5M learners with 2D video. Costs: static hosting, a managed database, on-device tracking: under $0.05 per family per month (estimate).
