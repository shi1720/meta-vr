# Signsprout | Learn your first ASL signs with your own two hands

Learning a sign from a video can leave you wondering whether your hands are right. Signsprout brings a patient practice partner into a small, seated space on Meta Quest.

Watch Sprout demonstrate a sign, follow glowing guide hands, try it from memory, and grow a plant for every sign you learn. Specific feedback helps you adjust your handshape, position and movement. The companion site adds a 3D dictionary, progress, phone pairing and practical lesson plans.

This video uses actual app footage from Meta's Immersive Web Emulator with a Quest 3 profile and simulated learner hands, plus browser footage with sample progress. Narration is synthesized; captions are included. It is not footage of physical headset testing. The deployed coach uses the no-cost built-in planner; Gemini support is optional.

Signsprout teaches first ASL vocabulary. It does not assess facial grammar and does not replace Deaf teachers or community. Paid Deaf educator review and testing with real signers on physical Quest hardware are planned before a wider launch.
